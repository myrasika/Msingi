Constitution of Kenya • Article 49

Your rights when arrested, detained or charged.

About Article 49

Article 49 protects your rights when you are arrested or detained. It ensures you are treated fairly and with dignity.

Key Clause 49(1)(f): Must be brought before a court as soon as reasonably possible, but not later than twenty-four hours after being arrested.
