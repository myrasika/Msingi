Article 40

Verified

Every person has the right, either individually or in association with others, to acquire and own property of any description and in any part of Kenya.

Article 40(3): Parliament shall not enact a law that permits the State or any person to arbitrarily deprive a person of property of any description.

Article 40(4): Provision may be made for compensation to be paid to occupants in good faith who may not hold title to land.
